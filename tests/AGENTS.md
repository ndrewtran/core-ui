# Test navigation

This directory owns cross-package and synthetic acceptance fixtures. Keep
fixtures explicit about the authority, positive path, negative path, and
evidence assertion they exercise. Tests prove facts but do not author product
meaning.

Use `pnpm check` for affected proof and `pnpm check:all` before milestone
evidence capture.

Before retaining evidence, follow `evidence/README.md`. Keep source, executed,
proof-tool, and evidence identities distinct and resolve applicability and
invalidation values from the owning evidence record and canonical authority.
